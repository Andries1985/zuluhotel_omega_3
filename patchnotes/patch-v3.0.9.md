# Patch Notes - v3.0.9
**Zuluhotel Omega 3 | Beta Shard**

**Date: [August 29, 2026]**

---

## What Changed

Patch 3.0.9 is a massive content patch: a brand-new Bulk Order Deed (BOD) system for all 8 crafting skills, roughly 30 new furniture/decor/tool item packages, a revived Rituals system with 6 new rituals and a new questline, Bowcraft split into its own dedicated crafting tool, precious-metal coin melting, and a long list of smaller fixes across cooking, combat, guilds, and begging.

## Bulk Order Deeds

- Every crafting skill (Alchemy, Blacksmithy, Bowcraft, Carpentry, Cooking, Inscription, Tailoring, Tinkering) now has a Bulk Order Deed system: craft items on request for a Small deed (skill 50+), or work toward a curated Large deed (skill 100+) built from a themed shopping list of items.
- Turn in filled deeds to any NPC of the matching craft for four separate rewards: points (redeemable for Omega Tokens), gold, and a mix of Fame and Karma.
- A universal Bulk Order Book holds up to 500 deeds and can be paged through in-game.
- Large deeds are built from 166 hand-curated named templates (e.g. "Platemail Regiment," "Crusader Swords," "Codex Damnorum Set A") rather than a fully random item draw, so completed Small deeds are actually tradeable toward a known set of Large targets.
- Higher-tier materials and Exceptional quality are rarer to roll on a Large deed, and pay out proportionally better.

### Player Impact

- A brand-new turn-in grind exists for every crafting skill, with real, tunable rewards.
- Points convert into the existing Omega Token vanity currency; gold and Fame/Karma scale with the material used to fill the order.

## New Item Packages

- Roughly 30 new packages of craftable and placeable items have been added, spanning furniture (chairs, benches, tables, pillows, tapestries, banners), lighting fixtures, clocks, gravestones, fountains, statues, engraving tools, celestial instruments (telescope, orrery, bronze globe), communication crystals, origami, and several themed decor packs (Gothic, Rustic, Shadow, Evil Home Decor, Toluno Furniture, King's Collection, and more).
- Statue crafting moved from Carpentry to Tinkering: statues are now made from clay, plus your choice of any wood log or metal ingot as a second material. Whichever material you use determines the statue's name and color, and requires the matching Carpentry or Blacksmithy skill.

### Player Impact

- A large amount of new decorative and functional content is now craftable or otherwise obtainable across most crafting skills.
- Statues are a new, unique two-material crafting category under Tinkering.

## Rituals Revived

- Ritual scrolls that previously did nothing when cast now actually work.
- Failed ritual attempts explain what went wrong (wrong spot, wrong facing, spoke too late, wrong words, interrupted, low mana) instead of silently fizzling.
- Reinforcement-style rituals (Hardening, Resilience, Blood Seeking, Vital Infusion, Quick Healing, and the three Ward rituals) now carry real risk that increases the more enchanted the item already is.
- Six new rituals: Demonstration (a safe, mana-free practice mode), Elemental Ward, Planard Ward, Vital Infusion, Perilous Theurgy (grants a random single-element Fury enchant), and Racial Theurgy (grants a Slayer enchant).
- New readable Ritual Chant Books reproduce a ritual's actual spoken lines.
- New Ritual Altar content: accept a quest from Arch-Mage Valthor, offer the right reagents at a ritual altar to summon a boss, defeat it for a trophy, and turn the trophy in for a reward.

### Player Impact

- Rituals are a fully functioning system for the first time, with clear feedback on failure.
- Six new rituals to learn and use, plus a new quest chain built around them.
- The game's first proper quest-journal system: accept quests from NPCs, track objectives, and turn in for rewards.

## Bowcraft & Crafting Quality of Life

- Bowcraft (bows, crossbows, arrows, bolts) now has its own dedicated crafting tool and a real graphical crafting menu, replacing the old scrolling text list.
- Bulk-crafting arrows and bolts is now a single-roll batch option.
- Crafting tools (blacksmith hammer, saw, tinker's tools, sewing kit, Bowcraft tool) can now be targeted directly at your Omega Cache to craft straight from stockpiled materials, without pulling them into your backpack first.

### Player Impact

- Bowcraft has a cleaner, faster crafting experience.
- Crafters using an Omega Cache no longer need to manually withdraw materials before crafting.

## Precious Metals

- The ore/ingot previously called Copper has been renamed to Orichalcum.
- You can now melt down large stacks of Gold, Silver, or Copper coins into real ingots using Tongs at a forge (500 gold, 1,000 silver, or 2,000 copper coins per ingot).

### Player Impact

- A new way to convert large coin stockpiles into a useful crafting resource.

## Fixes

- Cooking now works next to a house's built-in fireplace or oven, not just placed/crafted ones.
- Tinkering's Crystal Items category no longer requires standing near a Soulforge.
- Trash cans now play a sound when you drop items into them.
- Fire and Ice Arrows and Thunderbolts now render with the correct color; Thunderbolts also show the correct item graphic.
- Begging payouts are slightly larger and more variable, and now sometimes pay out in copper or silver instead of always gold.
- The guild color picker now offers nearly the full color range (up from about 150 preset colors), with pagination and a live preview on mannequins.
- A batch of items that were silently broken or missing (including several new decorative and crafting items) have been fixed so they work and appear correctly in the world.

### Player Impact

- Several small but frequently-hit annoyances are resolved.
- Guild leaders have vastly more color choices for their guild.

## Summary

- Added a full Bulk Order Deed system for all 8 crafting skills, with a 166-template Large deed catalog and a 4-currency reward system.
- Added roughly 30 new item packages (furniture, lighting, decor, tools) and moved statue crafting to Tinkering as a new clay + wood-or-ore combo craft.
- Revived the Rituals system (previously non-functional), added 6 new rituals, readable chant books, and a new Ritual Altar quest chain via the game's first quest-journal system.
- Gave Bowcraft its own dedicated crafting tool and menu, and added Omega Cache targeting for crafting tools.
- Renamed Copper ore/ingots to Orichalcum and added coin-to-ingot melting.
- Fixed cooking near built-in fireplaces/ovens, Tinkering's Soulforge gate, trash can sounds, elemental ammo colors/graphics, begging payouts, and expanded the guild color picker.

Thanks for playing Zuluhotel Omega 3.
