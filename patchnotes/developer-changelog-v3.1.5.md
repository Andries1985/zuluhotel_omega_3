## Detailed Changes By Theme

### 1. Kill credit: every kill records its killer (ZH2.5 1.1.5 port)

**Files involved:**
- `scripts/include/damages.inc`, `scripts/include/dotempmods.inc`, `pkg/opt/summoning/processpoisonmod.src`
- `pkg/systems/combat/poisonhit.src`, `pkg/systems/combat/poisononhit.src`, `scripts/control/poisoncontrol.src`, `scripts/items/walkon_pfield.src`, `scripts/ai/tamed.src`
- `pkg/opt/shilitems/vengeance.src`, `scripts/misc/chrdeath.src`

**Notable functional changes:**
- New `RecordKiller( who, by_who )` in `damages.inc` replaces the two `KilledBy` / `KilledBySerial` write pairs in `ApplyTheDamage`. It records only a mobile attacker; a blow with no attacker erases both props, so `by_who.serial` of a missing attacker is no longer saved as Uninit and an earlier killer is not credited.
- `SetPoison` gets a sixth parameter `credit := 0` (defaults to `caster`) and passes it to `processpoisonmod` as `parms[6]`; it no longer writes `PoisonedBy` itself.
- `processpoisonmod.src`: new `SetPoisonCredit( who, credit )` writes `PoisonedBy` + `PoisonedBySerial` only when a poisoning lands (fresh, or stronger than the current `psnDmg`). A landed poisoning with no poisoner, or a self-poisoning, erases both (review fix: `PoisonedBy` was left behind, and `scripts/misc/death.src` falls back to that name).
- New `PoisonTick( who, dmg, tick_type )` sends each tick through `ApplyTheDamage( who, poisoner, dmg, tick_type, 0 )` (a Thief caster adds `DMGID_NO_RESIST`). With no poisoner the tick stays `ApplyRawDamage`, but first runs the same `IsImmunedFromThisDamageType` test (review fix: a poison-immune NPC was immune to credited ticks and not to uncredited ones). The tick's own `StoreDamage` call and the dead `KilledBy` write are gone (`ApplyTheDamage` stores LottoDrops damage).
- Credit passed by: `poisonhit.src` (the wielder), `poisononhit.src` (the armour's wearer), `poisoncontrol.src` (the field's `Creator`, or nobody; the resist roll's stand-in is separate), `walkon_pfield.src` (the field's `Creator`), `tamed.src` (the pet, so its master gets it through `ApplyTheDamage`).
- `vengeance.src` includes `include/damages` and deals `ApplyTheDamage( victim, who, dmg, DMGID_MAGIC + DMGID_NO_RESIST )`; when the victim is the user that is self-damage, which `ApplyTheDamage` no longer cuts (theme 2).
- Mind Blast is unchanged. ZH3.0's already went through `ApplyTheDamage`, so unlike ZH2.5 its PvP damage stays as it was; ZH2.5's special case for a blast reflected onto its caster is not needed now that self-damage is uncut (theme 2).
- `chrdeath.src` erases the ghost's `KilledBySerial` after `RecordCharacterDeath` (it was read and never cleared). The corpse keeps its own copy.

**Expected impact:** poison (weapons, armour, fields, pets, spells) and Vengeance kills now credit the right player for kill credit, champion tallies, quests and loot. A later death no longer inherits an old killer.

### 2. PvP cuts: poison ticks and pets (ZH2.5 1.1.5 port)

**Files involved:**
- `scripts/include/damages.inc`, `pkg/systems/combat/include/hitscriptinc.inc`

**Notable functional changes:**
- `ApplyTheDamage` gets a fifth parameter `pvp_cut := 1`; 0 skips the global PvP slowdown. Only `PoisonTick` passes 0, because `processpoisonmod` already applies its own PvP factor to `psnDmg` (no double cut). All other callers are unchanged.
- `CalcPhysicalDamage`: an NPC attacker resolves to its master (`attacker.master`, falling back to `FindMaster()`) before the PvP melee test, so a player's pet, summon or Warrior for Hire hitting a player takes the same 0.5 melee cut as the player. Pets already took `ApplyTheDamage`'s 0.6 (its PvP test reads `by_who.master`), so pet melee on a player goes from 0.6 to 0.3 of its base, the same as a player's.
- The melee factor stays 0.5 (the owner's call, 2026-10-31). ZH2.5 uses 0.4 for all PvP melee.
- `ApplyTheDamage`'s PvP test now resolves an NPC attacker to `.master`, falling back to `FindMaster()` (the master CProp), like `CalcPhysicalDamage` (second review). A summon with only the CProp took the 0.5 melee cut but not the 0.6, so its spells and hit scripts were not cut at all.
- Self-damage (`by_who.serial == who.serial`) no longer takes the 0.6 (second review). It was cut before the function's own `by_who == who` raw branch. Callers: fire traps (`traps.src`), the backfire of cursed hit scripts (`manadrainscript`, `piercingscript`, `voidscript`, `invisibleonhit`), a Wrath of God turned on its caster (`wrathofgod.src`), Vengeance hitting its user, a reflected Mind Blast, and fields with no caster (theme 3). All now hit a player for full damage.

**Expected impact:** pets, summons and hirelings no longer bypass the PvP cuts against players. Poison ticks against players are not cut twice. Damage a player does to themselves is no longer cut.

### 3. Field scripts: location, caster and no-caster fallback (ZH2.5 1.1.5 port, review fixes)

**Files involved:**
- `scripts/control/firecontrol.src`, `scripts/control/poisoncontrol.src`, `scripts/control/paracontrol.src`, `scripts/control/energycontrol.src`

**Notable functional changes:**
- Each tick reads `item.realm` and x/y/z once and breaks when the realm is unset (the field was destroyed between a check and the lookup, which passed an uninit realm to `ListMobilesNearLocation`).
- All four read `ByTrueMage` and `Creator` (fire fields also `specialELE`, `FieldDice` and `Unresistable`) on every tick until `Creator` has been seen on two ticks, then keep them (second review: re-reading for the field's whole life was wasted work; the caster writes `Creator` first and the rest straight after, and ticks are 1-5 s apart). They look the caster up by its serial every tick with `SYSFIND_SEARCH_OFFLINE_MOBILES`, so a field notices a dead caster, and call `SetScriptController` once, when the caster is first found. The caster sets these props after `CreateItemAtLocation` has already started the script, so a field whose script ran first kept the defaults for its whole life and credited nobody. Fire fields did this in the port; poison fields got it from review finding #7; paralyze and energy fields were found on the 2026-10-31 notes pass and fixed the same way.
- With no caster (no `Creator`, or the caster can no longer be found, e.g. a dead monster) fire, poison and energy fields now use each victim as its own attacker. They used to make the FIRST victim the caster for everyone in the field, so one player was the attacker of every other player standing in it (review finding #2). Self damage goes through `ApplyTheDamage`'s self path: no credit, no hitlist, and no PvP cut (theme 2). The resist roll uses the victim's own skills, as the first victim's did before. Paralyze fields have no attacker and only feed `SmartAoE`.

**Expected impact:** fewer console errors from fields that expire mid-tick; fields made by a caster credit and target as that caster from the first tick; a field with no caster no longer blames a bystander.

### 4. Champfire (ZH2.5 d5d232a and 1.1.5 sections 35.1 / 35.3 / 35.4, review fix)

**Files involved:**
- `scripts/include/npccast.inc`, `scripts/include/npccastspells.inc`, `scripts/control/firecontrol.src`

**Casters:** Rikktor and Nyx the Seductress (`CProp IsMage i6`), The Summoner, the four Soul Whisperers and Sutek (no class), each with Magery and Eval Int 200.

**Notable functional changes:**
- `ChampFire()` assigned the element as strings ("EARTH"...). `GetProtLevel` only matches the element constants, so elemental protection never reduced champfire, and the field's `specialELE` never matched either. Now `EARTH` / `WATER` / `FIRE` / `AIR` / `NECRO` / `HOLY` constants; `SpecialChampFire`'s two `case` blocks match them (ZH3.0's hues kept).
- `SpecialChampFire`: the bolt is `RandomDiceStr( CHAMPFIRE_BOLT_DICE )` ("10d10+115", 125-215, about 170) instead of `CalcSpellDamage`; cursed equipment doubles the roll (it doubled the circle). New constant `CHAMPFIRE_FIELD_DICE` ("8d8+6", 14-70, about 42 a tick).
- The bolt and every Unresistable field call `ApplyElementalDamageNoResist`: the elemental protection of the rolled element is the only reduction. No `Resisted()` (no Magic Resistance roll, no class or Eval Int adjustment, no Magic Resistance gain) and no Mage caster pierce. Magic immunity items still block the bolt (`IsProtected()`), and hidden targets and no-PK areas are still skipped. The port first added an `unresistable` parameter to `ApplyElementalDamage` (as ZH2.5 did); review finding #9 replaced it, because that path still let a Mage champion cut the target's protection by 3 points per class level, and the owner wanted protection only. The parameter is gone and `spelldata.inc` is unchanged.
- Every one of the 21 champfire fields gets `Creator` (the caster's serial), `FieldDice` and `Unresistable 1`. Without a `Creator`, firecontrol made the first victim the caster. The creation block and the prop loop run under `set_critical` (second review): each `CreateItemAtLocation` starts its firecontrol, which hits before its first sleep, so a field that ran before the loop got 3d8, resisted, blaming the victim.
- `firecontrol.src` reads `ByTrueMage`, `specialELE`, `FieldDice`, `Unresistable` and `Creator` on every tick (theme 3). Fields without `FieldDice` still roll `power d8` and are resisted through `ApplyElementalDamage` as before.
- `GetSpellID` aliases `"meteorswarm"` (55) and `"gustoair"` (89) for template spellings (d5d232a); `cast_offensive_spell`'s template fallback reads `"::npcdesc"`.
- Not ported from d5d232a: its `config/npcdesc.cfg` spell-name renames (the aliases cover them) and three `config/animxlate.cfg` graphics (0x128, 0x15f, 0x160 under `MobileType Monster`; not checked against ZH3.0's file).

**Before (3.1.4), worked out from ZH3.0's code.** Averages; level 6 characters. Protection never applied. Bolt: `CalcSpellDamage` at circle 8 is 24d5 + Magery/5 (about 112), x2.5 for a Mage caster, /3 on a player; a Mage caster then "pierced" protection it didn't have, adding 18%; then `Resisted()` with the caster's Eval Int 200 and both sides' class modifiers. Field ticks: the victim was its own caster (3d8 with `ByTrueMage` read in time, 2d8 otherwise), then the PvP 0.6.

| Level 6 target | Bolt from Rikktor / Nyx | Bolt from Summoner, Soul Whisperers, Sutek | Field tick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior, 0 resist | ~545 | ~185 | ~20 (no Magery or Eval Int) |
| Paladin, 150 resist | ~89 | ~20 | depends on own skills |
| Mage, 150 resist | ~32 | ~7 | ~2 (150 Magery and Eval Int) |

**After (3.1.5), every class and every caster:**

| Protection in the rolled element | Bolt | Field tick (every 5 s) |
|---|---|---|
| 0% | ~170 (125-215) | ~42 (14-70) |
| 50% | ~85 | ~21 |
| 95% | ~9 | ~2 |
| 100%+ | heals | heals |
| Cursed equipment | x2 | - |
| Field after its champion dies | - | ~42 (victim as its own attacker; self-damage is not cut) |

ZH2.5's numbers differ: there Rikktor and Nyx still pierce protection (about 200 a bolt at 0%), and its Warrior class modifier in `Resisted()` is 1.75 where ZH3.0's is 2.5.

**Expected impact:** champfire hits the same whatever the champion's skills or the target's resist and class, and protection of the rolled element is the only defence. Champfire fields burn with their real element and credit the champion.

### 5. Champion spawns: kill tally, reward bags, last wave (ZH2.5 1.1.5 port, review fixes)

**Files involved:**
- `pkg/opt/champspawns/include/death.inc`, `pkg/opt/champspawns/include/rewards.inc`, `pkg/opt/champspawns/include/skulls.inc`, `pkg/opt/champspawns/scripts/control.src`

**Notable functional changes:**
- `ProcessChampionCorpse`: reads the altar first, pays `GiveRewardsToPlayers( altar )` and erases the tally, then rains gold and sleeps the 10 minutes. Before, the tally was erased after the sleep and never read.
- `GetAltarByCorpse` returns 0 when the corpse has no `altar` CProp, so a staff-spawned champion (templates carry `CProp Champion`) no longer passes an error around; it only rains gold.
- `RegisterChampionSpawnKill` rewritten: the altar must exist and be `active`; the killer is `KilledBySerial` or, failing that, the newest player on the corpse's hitlist (`FindChampionSpawnKiller`, `IsChampionCreditable`: players only, no staff); the entry is `{ kills, systime }`, written under `set_critical`; a non-dictionary tally is rebuilt and an old bare count is accepted.
- `rewards.inc`: restored `GiveRewardsToPlayers`, `GiveChampionBag`, `AddChampionReward` with constants `CHAMP_BAG_MIN_KILLS` 150, `CHAMP_CREDIT_WINDOW` 7200 s (last kill within 2 hours of the newest on the tally), `CHAMP_BAG_GRAPHIC` 0x0E76, `CHAMP_REWARD_COLOR` 53. Tiers add up: 150 kills = 1 Transcend Scroll, 1 vanity token, 1 present; 300 = +1 scroll, +1 token; 500 = +1 stat potion; 750 = +1 Power Tome; 1000 = +1 tome, +1 stat, +2 tokens, +1 present. One bag per character. Dead `EligibleForLoot` and the `:Events:bag` include removed; `include/random`, `:containers:storageAreas` and `include/constants/locations` added.
- A tally entry from before the patch (a bare kill count, no time) is paid without the 2-hour window (review finding #5: it used to pay nothing, so a spawn running across the deploy lost every earlier kill).
- Bag delivery (review finding #4): the bag is built and filled at `DEFAULT_LOCATION_ITEM_CREATION`, then moved to the backpack of an online player, or else to the bank box (`CP_GetStorageContainerForMobile( who, CP_BANK_AREA, CP_CREATE )`; ZH3.0 has no `FindBankBox`). Filling it inside the backpack lost whatever went over the backpack's limits. `AddChampionReward` returns how many items failed to create; failures (and failed presents) are logged with `SysLog`. A bag that fits in neither container stays at the creation spot, is logged with its serial, and an online player is told to page staff.
- `skulls.inc`: new `clearWhiteSkulls`, which `createNextRedSkull` now calls instead of its own copy of the wipe (review finding #8); `createNextWhiteSkull` no longer wipes before calling it (second review: the wipe ran twice). `control.src`: a stalled wave above 20% only clears the white skulls; at 20% or less it destroys the last red skull. It used to add a red skull and take it away again, which on the last wave marked the altar completed and spawned the champion early.

**Expected impact:** players with 150+ kills at a champion spawn get a Champion Reward Bag when the champion dies. The last wave can no longer finish early.

## Risk and Regression Notes

- **Champfire changes every matchup (theme 4).** A level 6 Mage with 150 resist goes from about 32 to 170 a Rikktor bolt at 0% protection, a Paladin from about 89; a Warrior with no resist goes from about 545 down to 170. Bolts from The Summoner, the Soul Whisperers and Sutek go from 7-185 to 170. Fields go from 2-20 a tick to 42. Elemental protection is now the only defence, and players will notice which elements they lack. Tune `CHAMPFIRE_BOLT_DICE` / `CHAMPFIRE_FIELD_DICE` in `npccastspells.inc`.
- **Champfire no longer trains Magic Resistance.** `Resisted()` used to award resist points.
- **A dead champion's fields keep burning (themes 3, 4).** For the rest of their 60 seconds each victim is its own attacker: still protection-only, still about 42 a tick (self-damage is not cut), and credited to nobody.
- **Fields look up their caster every tick (theme 3).** Fire (every 5 s), poison, paralyze and energy (every second) fields each do one `SystemFindObjectBySerial()` per tick, players' fields included; the CProps are read only until the caster has set them. A field with no `Creator` at all keeps re-reading them.
- **No-caster fields (theme 3).** A Mage standing in a fire field that has no caster is still its own Mage caster and pierces its own protection by 3 per class level, as the first victim did before. Rare: fields cast as spells carry a `Creator`.
- **Self-damage hits full (theme 2).** Fire traps, cursed hit-script backfires, a Wrath of God turned on its caster and fields with no caster lost the PvP 0.6 they took on players: about two-thirds more damage. Monsters are unaffected (the cut never applied to them).
- **Summons with only a master CProp take the PvP 0.6 (theme 2)** on spells and hit scripts against players, as tamed pets already did.
- **Poison is now an attack for repsys (theme 1).** Each credited tick runs `SetScriptController()` and the hit as the poisoner, so poisoning an innocent flags the poisoner for as long as it ticks, as melee does, and every tick on a monster adds the poisoner to its hitlist.
- **Magusbane and Magehunter resist more poison (theme 1).** Their `AttackTypeImmunities 639` includes poison, which raw ticks ignored. Every poison tick except a Thief's (`DMGID_NO_RESIST`) now does nothing to them, credited or not, traps included. No other template's immunities include poison.
- **Pet melee on players is halved (theme 2).** Pets, summons and Warriors for Hire now take the 0.5 melee cut on top of the 0.6 they already took: 0.3 of base, the same as a player. Pet spells and poison are unchanged. Tamers, summoners and Warrior for Hire owners in PvP will feel it.
- **Reward bags are new loot (themes 5, 6).** The corpse loses its tokens and presents and part of its tomes, scrolls and stat potions. Per character, no per-account limit: credit needs killing blows.
- **The two-hour window (theme 5)** runs from each player's last credited kill to the newest kill on the tally (the end of the last wave), not from the champion's death. Tune `CHAMP_CREDIT_WINDOW` in `rewards.inc`.
- **Altars mid-spawn at the deploy (theme 5).** A dictionary tally is kept; its bare counts pay without the window. A tally that isn't a dictionary (an error left by the old 10-minute erase) is rebuilt at the next kill.
- **Undeliverable bags (theme 5)** sit at the item creation spot (6336, 1580, 0, britannia_alt) and are logged with their serial and owner. Staff move them by hand.
- **The last wave is harder (theme 5).** A champion can no longer appear with the final wave about a fifth done.
- **Custom NPCs keep only their stored gear (theme 7).** With a backpack equipped before the AI starts, the AI setups' no-backpack branch no longer runs for them, so a template's `dress`, `equip` or `mount` is not added on top.
- **`modsetup.inc` now re-equips package-AI NPCs (theme 7).** With `::npcdesc` found, any package AI other than the Warrior for Hire that uses `modsetup.inc` is re-equipped from its `equip` template on each AI start, as script-AI NPCs already were.
- **Stacking (theme 7).** Items that differ only in an `IgnoreCprops` property now merge on the package paths too. Merges can't be undone.
- **An idle Warrior for Hire spars with its master on any damage credited to the master (theme 9; kept by the owner's call).** Outside a fight, a DAMAGED or ENGAGED event naming the master starts a spar, whether the master hit it directly or with an area spell, a field or their pet's poison (now credited to the master). With the sticky spar, it then stays on the master until "stop", even if a monster attacks it meanwhile.
- **`ResolveAttacker()` picks the first match (theme 9).** If a player and their own pet both attack a warrior and the player isn't the warrior's opponent, the warrior answers the pet.
- **City vendors cost more (theme 10).** 50,000 a month (about 1,666 a day) comes out of each city vendor's gold; unpaid wages become debt, and after two months of debt the vendor closes.
- **One account, several votes (theme 14).** Every citizen character votes, so an account with several characters in a town has several votes, and a town's population (and the 3-member election minimum) can come from one account. A vote cast before the deploy keeps blocking every character on that account until that vote ends.
- **Old citizen lists convert on first read (theme 14).** A listed account whose characters no longer carry the town's `town` CProp drops out at that point. Not tested against live data.
- **Ranger balance (theme 11).** Taming and resurrection get easier with level; level 1-2 rangers fully heal pets less often than before (3% and 6% against 8.3%), level 6 more (18%).
- **`.akill <range> credit` records the staff member as the killer (theme 12).** Besides the tally, `scripts/misc/death.src` reads that `KilledBySerial`. A testing tool, logged with `LogCommand`.
- **Boss mass specials skip bystanders and safe areas (theme 13)**, for wild casters too: a player standing in a safe area is no longer hit by Massgust or champfire from outside it.
- **Bards of level 1-5 lose a point (theme 10)** on the level 7 chest roll; the old `<=` had given them one extra percent.
